About this model

Concept A for the Mae Nam project, Koh Samui: three 4 × 9 m loft bungalows with black metal walls, and a two-storey main house with two 2-bedroom apartments, around the existing pool. Lighting is baked in Blender (Cycles) for 15 March, 16:30.

Existing (chip at the top) shows the plot as found: the timber house on posts, the raised pool and the garden.

A concept visualisation, not a construction drawing. Furniture and planting are indicative. Neighbours, road and palms are approximate.
